mPredictor · DGNM

Tamil Nadu DGNM (Diploma in General Nursing & Midwifery): your chance of a government college seat, based on 2025 counselling results.

How this works
  • For each college, it finds the last community rank allotted to your community in 2025 (all 3 rounds, regular seats only).
  • 2026 has more government seats, so that cutoff is scaled by the college's 2026 ÷ 2025 seat ratio to give a projected 2026 cutoff.
  • Likely: your rank is within 90% of the projected cutoff. Borderline: within ±10%. Unlikely: beyond that.
  • Special quotas (Ex-servicemen, Sports, PWD, IRT Ward) and self-financing colleges are not included.
  • This is an estimate from one year of data, not an official prediction. Actual cutoffs depend on applicants, choices and drop-outs.
  • No candidate names or application numbers are used or stored on this site. Only seat counts and cutoff ranks.
