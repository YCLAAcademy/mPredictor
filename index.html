DGNM

DGNM (Nursing Diploma), Tamil Nadu. Find your chance for a government college seat, and in which round. Based on 2025 counselling results.

Enter your COMMUNITY RANK (last column in the rank list). Do not enter the overall RANK.

How it works
  • Yes = good chance. Maybe = small chance. No = not likely.
  • We use past counselling data (2025): the last rank that got a seat, for your community, in each college and round.
  • 2026 has more government seats. So we increase the 2025 last rank a little, to guess the 2026 last rank.
  • This is only a prediction. It is not official. Real results can change.
